Pit Boss kiosk

Learn. Find. Grill.

A touchscreen for the Pit Boss section in the store, so a shopper can find the right grill without waiting for help.

Project
Dansons × ASU
Year
2024
Role
UI designer
Format
In-store touchscreen, 1366 × 1024

01The grill sat on the floor and taught no one

In the store, the Pit Boss section had a help button that called a worker who often took a while to arrive. A shopper could walk past a very good grill and never learn what it does.

The kiosk does the explaining a salesperson isn't there to do. It opens with three choices: help me choose, check stock, or get more help.

Learn

Help me choose

Find

Which nearby stores have it, and how far away they are.

Grill

Need more help

Calls someone over, and tells the shopper help is on the way.

02A 30-second quiz

Five questions, each answered with one tap on a big card: how often you grill, what fuels your fire, how many people you cook for, what you want to try, and your budget.

Photos do the work wherever they can, so nobody has to know the difference between a pellet grill and a combo before they start.

03Our pitmaster recommends

The quiz ends with two grills, their prices and a shop button. A QR code on each lets the shopper take the details home, and a pop-up offers a discount on accessories after checkout.

04Check stock at your local store

If the grill isn't on the floor, the kiosk shows which nearby stores have it, how far away they are and when they close. And if it's not in stock anywhere, it can be bought in store and delivered.
